Property Manager
================

To start: double-click "Start Property Manager". The app opens in your web browser.
Keep the black window that appears open while you use the app; close it to stop the app.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
The first time you start it, a short setup asks for your business name and your properties.

Your information is kept in your Documents folder, in "Property Manager".
Back up that folder (for example to OneDrive or a USB drive) to keep a copy.

To update to a newer version, replace this folder with the new one. Your information stays in Documents.
